Emotion Design static clone

1. Unzip this folder onto your Desktop.
2. Open a terminal in the "site" folder and run:
   python3 -m http.server 8766
3. Visit http://127.0.0.1:8766/

compare-screenshots/ holds live-vs-clone images.

This zip is the local copy. The cloud agent was left in place until you confirm this file is on your computer.
