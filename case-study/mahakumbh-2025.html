Located beside Bade Hanuman Ji Mandir, it featured 13+ thematic zones connected by the narrative “Tirth – A Spiritual Crossing.” From projection mapping and VR to LiDAR murals and tactile artefacts, every element was designed to deliver a culturally rich, emotionally resonant experience.
