We created a fully immersive Digital Experience Centre at Maha Kumbh 2025, blending spatial design, original multimedia content, and interactive technology. Located beside Bade Hanuman Ji Mandir, it featured 13+ thematic zones connected by the narrative “Tirth – A Spiritual Crossing.” From projection mapping and VR to LiDAR murals and tactile artefacts, every element was designed to deliver a culturally rich, emotionally resonant experience.
